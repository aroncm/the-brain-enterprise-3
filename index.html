BASEBALL brAIn

The pitching change, measured in real time.

Baseball brAIn scores every pitch of every Major League Baseball game, weighs the starter against the bullpen behind him, and marks the moment a pitching change is warranted. Club staff see it live, in replay, and in their inbox.

  • Live Triggers. During games, the model tracks each starter pitch by pitch and escalates through Watch, Prep, and Pull Now.
  • Game Replays. A pitch-by-pitch review of every game: how the model's read developed and where the trigger fired.
  • Game Briefings. A morning email covering each game's pitching decisions and the moments that mattered.
